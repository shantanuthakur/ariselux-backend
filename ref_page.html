Qube Power Mini

The QUBEpower Mini is a compact and versatile light tower featuring an air-cooled 1-cylinder Honda engine running at 3000 rpm and powered by a 4-liter petrol tank. Its portability and adaptability make it ideal for emergencies and small-scale work; it easily mounts on the bed of trucks or tractors, providing essential illumination in various scenarios.

Mast height

10 ft (3 m)

Light

4 x 100 W LED

Runtime

10 hours

Light coverage

350 sq. m

FEATURES

  • Ideal for emergency lighting or small and more focused lighting requirements
  • Easy to handle and can be pushed by an individual
  • Easy to transport inside passenger vehicles like jeeps and small vans
  • Can be used as a small genset

HIGHLIGHTS

  • Unique 100 W LED modules with special lenses to optimize light distribution
  • Each module is adjustable individually for better light orientation
  • IP65-rated LEDs can work in any weather
  • Lightweight plated mast
  • Brake ratchet winch raises the tower in < 30 sec
  • Self-lubricating inserts provide a smooth and low-friction movement of tower segments
  • Tower retracts to a compact size, thereby allowing for easy transport
  • 2 solid rubber wheels mounted to the frame
  • Lightweight machine allows easy movement by a single person using the retractable handle
  • Machine can also be lifted by 2 people using the two provided handles
  • Compact size of the machine ensures it can be transported in pickup trucks and vans
  • Outriggers can be extended to provide support for the light tower
  • The machine is good for wind speeds up to 50 kph
  • Power output of 300 W is available through a 3-pin industrial female socket that is IP65 rated
  • Power can be supplied to run the equipment through a 3-pin industrial male socket
  • Changeover switch allows switching of power supply between the genset and grid supply

Best For

  • Emergency services
  • Plant maintenance and repairs
  • Boundary wall construction
